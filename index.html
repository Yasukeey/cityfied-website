How to apply

From Application to Cash in Hand

A straightforward process designed around your schedule. Most clients complete these stages within a single working day.

  1. Reach out

    Call, WhatsApp, or walk into any of our branches and tell us what your business needs.

  2. Apply & share documents

Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to get a loan?

We aim to process complete loan applications within 24 hours, subject to appraisal, verification and approval. The faster your documents are ready, the faster we can respond.

Do I need to belong to a borrowing group?

No. Cityfied Capital lends to individuals. You qualify on your own merit and business, not through a group.

How do I repay my loan?

Repayments are made in small weekly or monthly instalments, whichever suits your cash flow, conveniently from your mobile phone. There are no account maintenance fees.

How do I receive the money?

Once your loan is approved, funds are disbursed directly to your mobile phone. No need to queue or open a new account.

What documents do I need to apply?

A valid National ID and basic records of your business. Our team will let you know if anything else is needed during appraisal.

Where are you located?

Our head office is at Spur Mall, 2nd Floor, along Thika Super Highway, Ruiru. We also serve clients around CBD/Kasarani, Roysambu/Githurai, Ruiru/Juja, Thika and Kiambu/Githunguri. See the branch list above.
